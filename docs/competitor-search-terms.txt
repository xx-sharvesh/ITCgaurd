====================================================================
WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN ANY RESULT (so a new competitor is easy to rule
in or out fast)
====================================================================
1. Do they compare COST OF PAYING vs COST OF HOLDING in rupees,
   for a specific vendor, with the reasoning shown?
2. Do they reference Section 43B(h) Income-tax Act AND GST law
   in the SAME recommendation — not as two separate features?
3. Do they track a vendor's compliance against THIS BUYER'S OWN
   invoice history specifically, with a trend across months —
   not just a generic public GSTIN filing score?
4. Do they state a zero-false-positive matching guarantee, or do
   they just say "AI-powered" without specifics?

If a product does NOT do at least #1 and #3, it is a reconciliation
tool, not a real competitor to the decision layer — note it as
"category noise," not a direct threat.
